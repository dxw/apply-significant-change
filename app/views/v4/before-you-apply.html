Before you apply

If you have not started a consultation

Before you apply to make a significant change you should have completed a 3 week consultation or have one in progress. Guidance on how to run a consultation is available.

{{ govukInsetText({ text: "If you have not started or completed a consultation, you should not apply." }) }}

If you have already made the significant change

A significant change should not be made before an application has been approved.

{{ govukInsetText({ text: "If you have already made the significant change you should speak to your local DfE delivery officer before you apply." }) }}

They will advise you on what to do next.
