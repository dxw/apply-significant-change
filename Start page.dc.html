Apply to make a tier 1 significant change

Use this service to apply to make one or multiple tier 1 significant changes to a single academy.

A tier one significant change is:

  • increasing or decreasing existing mainstream or SEN pupil places
  • adding or removing a provision
  • adding or removing a satellite site or move the academy to another site
  • changing the gender composition of the academy
  • changing the age range of the academy
  • changing the type of SEN provision
  • adding or removing boarding provision
Increasing or decreasing mainstream pupil places

If you're increasing or decreasing mainstream places, a threshold applies.

Adding 31 or more mainstream places is a tier 1 significant change.

The threshold is cumulative. This means that if places have been added in the past, this will need to be taken into account

Increasing or decreasing SEN pupil places

If you're increasing or decreasing SEN places, a threshold applies.

Adding 21 or more places or a 20% increase in the current number of places (whichever is smaller) is a tier 1 significant change.

The threshold is cumulative. This means that if places have been added in the past, this will need to be taken into account

Adding or removing mainstream or SEN provision

If you are adding a new provision or removing one completely, it is often a tier 1 significant change.

It is a significant change to add:

  • a sixth form
  • boarding
  • a SEN unit
  • a resourced provision

Adding a new nursery is not a tier 1 significant change.

It is a significant change to remove:

  • a sixth form
  • boarding
  • a SEN unit
  • a resourced provision
  • a nursery
Adding or removing a satellite site or moving an academy to another site

Adding or removing a satellite site is always a tier 1 significant change.

If the academy is moving to another site, it's not a significant change if the new site is adjacent to the existing site.

Who can use this service

Only academy trusts should use this service. Local authorities should not use this service, even if the change or changes are being proposed at their request.

To use this service, you must be the:

  • chair of the academy trust
  • CEO of the academy trust
  • accounting officer of the academy trust

Before you start

Before you start using this service, it's important to read the guidance on making a significant change.

You must have completed a 3 week consultation or be in the process of running one.

When you're using the service, you'll need to upload some documents. These include:

  • evidence that there are no objection to the change or changes by a local authority
  • evidence of consultation outcomes if the consultation is complete

If you require consent from a religious body to make the change or changes, you will also need to upload evidence you have this.

After you apply

After the application has been submitted, a DfE delivery officer will contact you. They might need to ask for more information to process the application.
